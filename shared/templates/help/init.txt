{{usage}}

Selects the dialects this tree will compile and install. Repeat --dialect
once per provider. Re-running replaces the previous set. --all restores
every dialect. Does not take --connection.

  --dialect  Dialect to enable. Repeat for each dialect. Aliases from this file apply.
  --all      Enable every dialect. Restores the committed full-provider set.

Examples:
  {{command}} --dialect sqlite --dialect postgres
  {{command}} --dialect oracle
  {{command}} --all
